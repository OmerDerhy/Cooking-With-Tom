# Cooking With Tom - Kids Game 🐼👨‍🍳

Welcome to **Cooking With Tom**, a fun, interactive 2D cooking game built in Unity! In this game, players step into a lively kitchen to prepare delicious meals and treats for their hungry friend, Tom the Panda. 

## 🌟 Features

* **Interactive Kitchen Tools**: Players can prepare food using a variety of interactive appliances:
  * 🍳 **Pan & Pot**: Sizzle and boil your ingredients to perfection.
  * ♨️ **Oven & Microwave**: Bake and heat up meals.
  * 🥤 **Blender**: Mix up some refreshing juices and smoothies.
* **Level Progression**: Complete levels by preparing and serving the exact sequence of foods Tom requests. Win levels to unlock new challenges!
* **In-Game Economy**: Earn coins for every level completed (with a 200-coin bonus for first-time completions!). 
* **Market & Customization**: Visit the in-game shop to spend your hard-earned coins and unlock new, fun skins for Tom and other items.
* **Recipe Book**: A built-in recipe manager helps players discover and learn how to make various dishes.
* **Endless Mode**: Keep the fun going and see how many orders you can fulfill in the endless game mode!
* **Monetization & Rewards**: Features rewarded ads and in-app purchases (IAP) to unlock premium skins or get extra coins.

## 🛠️ Technical Details

* **Engine**: Unity 3D
* **Language**: C#
* **Platforms**: PC, iOS, Android (Contains mobile-ready SDKs like Unity Ads and Unity IAP).

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Check the Order**: Watch the thought bubble to see what food Tom is craving.
2. **Prepare the Food**: Select the right ingredients and use the correct kitchen appliance (Oven, Pan, Blender, etc.) to cook them.
3. **Serve Tom**: Drag the finished food to Tom to feed him.
4. **Win Coins**: Complete the level successfully to earn coins, unlock new levels, and buy cool skins!
